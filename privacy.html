Pulselyzer Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information in the Pulselyzer app.

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pulselyzer ("Application", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Application.

1. Service Purpose

Pulselyzer is an AI-powered message analysis application that processes content voluntarily provided by users to generate insights, summaries, and statistics.

2. Information We Process

  • Exported chat files or message content uploaded by the user.
  • Analysis preferences and application settings.
  • Technical and performance information required for app functionality and stability.

The Application does not secretly access third-party accounts, private communications, or devices without user action.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Messaging), RevenueCat (subscriptions), and Meta/Facebook App Events to operate features, monitor reliability, and improve the service. These providers may process limited technical or usage data under their own privacy terms.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. We may share limited data with service providers that are necessary for app operation, analytics, crash reporting, subscriptions, and legal compliance.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

6. User Controls

Users may delete uploaded content and request data deletion where applicable.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Application after updates means acceptance of the revised policy.
