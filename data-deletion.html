Pulselyzer Data Deletion Request

How users can delete data associated with the Pulselyzer app.

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Pulselyzer app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account / Delete My Data and confirm.

This removes locally stored imported chats and preferences on your device and resets associated server-side goals and notification preferences tied to your app profile. Subscription and credit balances may be retained as described below.

Alternative request method

If you cannot access the app, send a request to veysburak011@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include your Member ID from Settings if available.

What is deleted

  • Imported chat analysis data stored on your device.
  • App preferences and goal tracking data on our servers linked to your device identifier.
  • Push notification tokens and referrer metadata associated with your profile.

What may be retained

  • Subscription and payment records required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, and accounting obligations.
  • Minimal technical logs required for security and abuse prevention for a limited retention period.
  • Credit transaction identifiers needed to prevent duplicate billing credits.

Retention period

Deletion requests are processed within a reasonable period. Certain records may be retained for up to 90 days where required for legal or operational reasons.
