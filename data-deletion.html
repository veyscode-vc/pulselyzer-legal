Pulselyzer Data Deletion Request

How users can delete data associated with the Pulselyzer app.

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Pulselyzer app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account / Delete My Data and confirm.

Alternative request method

If you cannot access the app, send a request to support@pulselyzer.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

What is deleted

  • Imported chat analysis data and app preferences associated with your app profile.
  • Locally stored app data on your device when deletion is confirmed in-app.

What may be retained

  • Subscription and payment records required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, and accounting obligations.
  • Minimal technical logs required for security and abuse prevention for a limited retention period.

Retention period

Deletion requests are processed within a reasonable period. Certain records may be retained for up to 90 days where required for legal or operational reasons.
